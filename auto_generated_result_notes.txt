Recommended conclusion:
The project should emphasise known-patient monitoring as the main application, while presenting new-patient generalisation as a stricter robustness test. Weak or negative R2 under Scenario B should be interpreted as evidence of limited unseen-patient generalisation, not as a project failure. Dataset 2 and the merged analyses strengthen the project by adding source-aware supplementary evidence and cross-source stress tests rather than being forced into the main model.
